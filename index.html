Do you keep rehearsing the same conversation in your head over and over again, but still can’t bring yourself to have it with the person who needs to hear it?

What makes this different

This isn’t just about one conversation—it’s about a pattern.

Obviously this one conversation does matter quite a bit, but this work will help you see clearly if you’re regularly avoiding conflict, chronically putting other people’s feelings before your own, or putting things off until you snap at people. The goal here isn’t to become fearless or perfectly calm, it’s just to stop letting your fear run the show.

WWe’ll take the conversation you keep rehashing and slow it down. We’ll work out what you’re really trying to say, what you’re scared will happen, and where you’re most likely to get nervous and back down or over-explain. Then, we’ll find our way to a version of the conversation that feels honest, clear, and actually possible for you! So that when the moment comes, you’re not winging it or rambling in panic. You know what matters and you know what needs to be said.

What you walk away with

Not just clarity. A way forward.

You'll have a framework for what to do the next time you feel stuck, in addition to insight into why you got stuck in the first place.

You'll know how to look at what you want through the lens of what actually matters to you, so the decision stops feeling impossible and starts feeling like yours.

And you'll understand the fear well enough to stop letting it make decisions for you.
