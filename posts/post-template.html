On identity

What does it actually mean to lose yourself — and how do you find your way back?

April 2025  ·  5 min read

It doesn't happen all at once. It happens in a thousand small moments where you said yes when you meant no, or stayed quiet when you had something to say. And one day you look up and don't quite recognize the life you're living.

You're not unhappy, exactly. You're just... somewhere slightly outside yourself. Going through the right motions in the right life, and yet.

Why it's so hard to name

The hardest part of this particular kind of loss is that it doesn't have a clean cause. Nothing went wrong. Nobody did anything terrible. You just — drifted. And because everything looks fine from the outside, it feels ungrateful to say it out loud.

"Everything is fine. I just feel off."

If you've ever said that — to a friend, to a therapist, to yourself at midnight — you know exactly what I mean. And you also know that "fine" can be its own kind of ache.

Where to start

You start by getting curious instead of critical. Not "what is wrong with me" but "what is actually true for me right now, underneath all the performing?"

That is not a question you can answer by thinking harder. It's a question you answer by talking — out loud, with someone who is genuinely listening and asking the right things back.
