About

Hi, I'm Steph.

I'm a communication and decision coach. I work with people who have built a full life — the career, the relationship, the kids, the house — and still feel like something is off. Not broken. Just off.

"I love my life. I just don't feel like myself in it anymore."

If that sentence stopped you — you're exactly who I built this practice for.

Here's the honest version of how I became a coach: I kept understanding my own patterns perfectly and changing absolutely nothing. I could explain exactly why I was doing the thing. Trace it back. Name it clearly. Tell my friends about it in a way that made complete sense.

And then do it again the next week.

Insight without support has a ceiling. I found mine. And once I got actual support — not advice, not frameworks, just honest conversation with someone who wasn't going to let me off the hook — things started to shift. That's what I do for other people now.

I'm not here to tell you what to do. I'm here to be the person who helps you hear yourself again — and actually do something with what you hear.

How I work

I'm in it with you. Not above it.

01

I ask, I don't prescribe

I don't come in with a plan for your life. I ask the questions that help you figure out what you actually want — separate from what you've been told you should want.

02

Honest, not performative

No scripts. No "have you tried journaling?" energy. Just direct conversation that gets to the root of what's going on — and what you want to do about it.

03

I still have a coach

I'm not coaching from a mountain. I have hard weeks too. I still catch myself narrating a pattern really clearly and then doing it anyway. The difference is I don't stay there as long anymore. That's the work.
