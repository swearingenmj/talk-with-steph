About

Hi, I'm Steph.

I'm a communication and decision coach. I work with people who have built a full life — the career, the relationship, the kids, the house — and still feel like something is fundamentally off. Like they've lost the thread of who they actually are.

"I love my life. I just don't feel like myself in it anymore."

If that sentence stops you mid-scroll, you are exactly who I built this practice for.

I became a coach because I believe that the most important conversations we have are the ones we keep avoiding. The ones we have with ourselves about what we actually want. The ones we need to have with the people we love. The ones that feel too risky to start and too costly not to.

My work is built around two things: helping you get clear on who you are and what you want, and giving you the tools to communicate that honestly — to yourself and to the people in your life.

I'm not here to tell you what to do. I'm here to help you hear yourself again.

How I work

Honest, unhurried, and always on your side.

01

No prescriptions

I don't tell you what to do. I ask the questions that help you figure out what you actually want, separate from what you think you should want.

02

Real conversations

Not scripts. Not frameworks. Just honest, direct dialogue that gets to the root of what's actually going on — and what you want to do about it.

03

No disruption required

This isn't about blowing up your life. It's about understanding it more clearly and learning to live it more honestly, on your own terms.
