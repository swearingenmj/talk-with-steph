Common questions

What to expect.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching is forward-focused — we're not going back to diagnose the past, we're working on who you want to be and how you want to live. If therapy is something you need, I'll say so.

How long does coaching take?

That depends entirely on what you're working through. Some people find clarity in a handful of sessions. Others choose to work together over several months. We figure that out together.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

That's exactly what the free session is for. You don't need to have it figured out before we talk. Most people don't. That's the point.

Do you only work with women?

No. My work is designed for all people navigating the particular kind of quiet identity loss that tends to come with building a full life for everyone around you. If you're wondering if this is for you, reach out and let's talk.

Not sure where to start?

Start with a free session. We'll figure it out together.
