Common questions

What to expect.

Is this therapy?

No — and I'll be direct about the difference. Therapy often goes back to understand the past. Coaching is about what you want to do now. Both are valuable. If I think therapy is what you need, I'll tell you. I'm not going to take your money and pretend coaching is the answer to everything.

How long does coaching take?

Honestly, it depends on what you're working through. Some people get what they need in a few sessions. Others want to keep going. There's no package I'm trying to sell you into — we figure it out as we go.

What if I don't know what I need?

"I don't know what I need" is probably the most common thing I hear on discovery calls. You don't need to have it figured out before we talk. That's literally what the call is for.

Do you only work with women?

No. I work with anyone navigating the specific kind of quiet drift that comes from spending years building a life for everyone around you. If you're wondering whether this is for you, just reach out. Worst case, we have a good conversation.

Not sure where to start?

Good. That's exactly what the first call is for. No prep, no pitch — just a real conversation.
