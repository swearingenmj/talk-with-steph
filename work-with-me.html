Will this actually work for me?

If you're ready to get past whatever is blocking you, I'm happy to help. That's genuinely all there is to it.

What I can tell you is that this isn't talk therapy, and it isn't just another self-reflection exercise. It's practical, it moves quickly, and the goal is always the same: to get you from stuck to moving. If you're not sure whether it's the right fit, the discovery call is a no-commitment way to find out before you spend anything.

What a session actually looks like

You show up and tell me what's going on, and we figure out what's underneath it — the real fear, the real want, the values that are in conflict — and we build something concrete from that.

By the end of each session you'll have something you didn't have at the start: a clearer picture of what you actually want, a better understanding of what's been in the way, or a plan for the conversation you need to have. Usually all three.
